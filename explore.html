Tiger

The tiger is a powerful and majestic big cat known for its striking orange coat with black stripes. As a top predator, it plays a crucial role in maintaining the balance of its ecosystem. Tigers are solitary, strong, and highly territorial animals, often symbolizing strength, courage, and mystery. Sadly, many tiger species are endangered due to habitat loss and poaching, making conservation efforts vital for their survival.

Lion

The lion, often called the "king of the jungle," is a symbol of strength, courage, and leadership. Known for its golden mane and powerful roar, the lion lives in groups called prides, showing both social bonds and hunting skills. Native to Africa and parts of Asia, lions are apex predators, playing a vital role in their habitats. Like many wild animals, they face threats from habitat loss and human conflict, making their conservation important.
